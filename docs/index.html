⚡ What Happens Behind the Scenes?

  • 1
    You tap or type from anywhere

    Over cellular (5G/4G) or Wi-Fi. Message is sent instantly via Telegram's secure HTTPS cloud.

  • 2
    MacBook receives via Long-Polling

    Your Mac makes outbound HTTPS requests. No router firewall openings, static IPs, or VPNs required.

  • 3
    Native macOS Engine Executes

    Wakes frame buffer for crisp Retina screenshots, drives Finder via AppleScript, or delegates to Antigravity AI.

  • 4
    Direct Delivery to Your Phone

    Images, files, and AI results appear directly in your Telegram chat within seconds.

Remote Finder & File Manager

Open Finder at any directory, list recent files, or reveal specific items. Browse your Mac from your phone as if you were sitting right in front of it.

Google Drive Cloud Sync

Copy any file or directory into your Mac's Google Drive sync folder. Google Drive immediately uploads it, making it ready on your Android phone in seconds.

Google Antigravity AI Bridge

Harness the full power of Antigravity AI on your Mac. Send complex programming, debugging, and file organization prompts straight to the Antigravity CLI.

Smart Clamshell Sleep Override

Leave your MacBook plugged into power with the lid closed. MacRemote prevents idle and closed-lid sleep assertions so your Mac remains active 24/7.

Get Started in 3 Simple Steps

No complicated network settings. You will be up and running in less than 3 minutes.

Step 1

Create your free Telegram Bot

1. Open the Telegram app on your Android phone.

2. Search for @BotFather and tap Start.

3. Send: /newbot

4. Choose a display name (e.g. My MacBook) and a bot username (e.g. my_mac_remote_bot).

5. Copy the HTTP API Token provided (e.g. 792837492:AAF...).

Step 2

Run the automated installer on your Mac

Open Terminal on your Apple Silicon Mac and paste this 1-line command:

git clone https://github.com/ard-tube/mac-remote-agent.git ~/mac-remote-agent && cd ~/mac-remote-agent && ./setup.sh

The interactive script will prompt for your token, configure sleep prevention on AC power, and set up the background 24/7 service.

Step 3

Pair your Android Phone

1. Open your new bot on Telegram from your phone and tap Start.

2. Send the pairing command shown in your terminal:

/pair 2551

3. You will immediately receive a success message with interactive buttons! You're ready to control your Mac from anywhere!
